# Impeccable Design Context — Florencia Orquesta Band

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary audience**: Event organizers in Costa Rica — couples planning weddings, families organizing quinceañeras, HR managers for corporate events, parents for graduations. Most are in San Carlos / Alajuela / Zona Norte.

**Context of use**: Browsing on phone or desktop, comparing bands, making a significant emotional and financial decision. They arrive excited about their event but anxious about choosing right.

**Jobs to be done**:
- Evaluate whether FOB has the style, repertoire, and professionalism for their event
- Feel confident enough to make contact / send an inquiry
- Understand who the musicians are and what they play

**Emotional state at arrival**: Hopeful and somewhat overwhelmed — they want to be convinced, not sold to. They need to *feel* the band before they call.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Elegante, nocturno, cálido — refined/elegant, nighttime/nocturnal, warm

**Voice**: Like a premium entertainment agency — professional and clear, but never cold. The warmth of tropical music wrapped in a tailored suit.

**Emotional goal**: Make the visitor feel like they're already at the event — 9pm, warm amber lights, the first notes of a salsa hitting the dance floor. That feeling of *this is the one*.

**Anti-references**: Generic music website templates. Party flyer aesthetics. Neon club vibes. Stock-photo-heavy agency sites. Bootstrap-era grid layouts.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme**: Dark — confirmed. Events are afternoons and evenings; the site should feel like nighttime.

**Color**: Warm golds, deep oranges, and dark reds from the FOB logo. OKLCH-tinted deep surfaces (not pure black). No cyan, no purple, no neon.

**Typography direction**: Look for a display font that feels like a venue marquee or a premium event program — something with a crafted, slightly editorial quality. Pair with a clean, readable body font. Not serif-heavy (too classical), not mono (too tech). Something that lives between editorial and signage.

**References**: Premium entertainment agencies — professional, conversion-oriented, with large imagery and clear hierarchy. Think of how a luxury wedding photographer or a jazz club with real prestige presents itself.

**NOT**: AI default dark mode (glowing accents, purple-blue gradients). Gradient text. Side-stripe borders. Card grids of equal-sized boxes. Bounce animations.

### Design Principles
1. **Every page element should feel like 9pm** — nocturnal warmth, amber light, the moment before the music starts
2. **Trust through restraint** — premium means knowing what NOT to show. Generous whitespace beats packed content
3. **Music and musicians are the heroes** — the content (who they are, what they play) earns center stage
4. **Emotion before conversion** — make the visitor feel before they click. The CTA comes after the feeling
5. **Suit, not sequins** — joyful but composed. A little warmth goes a long way; avoid excess
